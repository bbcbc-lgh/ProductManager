# Design Interview Prompts

Translate answers into decisions rather than repeating adjectives.

- Pick two or three contrasting directions: for example restrained editorial, high-contrast industrial, or warm human utility.
- For density, ask what users scan per minute and whether comparison or focus dominates.
- For typography, define display, body, metadata, numeric, and fallback roles.
- For color, define semantic roles for background, surface, text, action, warning, success, and destructive states.
- For motion, define purpose, duration range, reduced-motion behavior, and what must remain immediate.
- For responsive behavior, define what disappears, stacks, scrolls, or changes priority on small screens.
- For accessibility, define contrast, focus, keyboard, labels, error, and touch-target expectations.
