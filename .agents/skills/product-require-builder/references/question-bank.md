# Requirement Interview Bank

Use selectively; do not ask every question when the answer is already known.

## Problem and users

- Who has the problem, in what situation, and what do they do today?
- What evidence says the problem matters now?
- Who is the primary user, secondary user, and non-user affected by the product?

## Outcome and scope

- What observable outcome proves the product helped?
- What is the smallest useful version?
- Which tempting features are explicitly out of scope for this release?

## Journeys and rules

- What is the first successful journey from entry to outcome?
- What can go wrong at each important step, and what should the user see?
- What permissions, states, limits, validation, or business rules apply?

## Data and operations

- What data is created, read, changed, exported, retained, or deleted?
- What integrations, environments, roles, uptime, latency, and scale matter?
- What is the migration, support, or rollback expectation?

## Acceptance

- What exact scenarios should pass?
- Which requirements are must-have, should-have, or deliberately deferred?
- What would make the owner reject the implementation even if it compiles?
